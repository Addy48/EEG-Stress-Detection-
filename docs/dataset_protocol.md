# SAM-40 Experimental Protocol & Task Specifications

## Dataset Overview
- **Dataset:** SAM-40 (Stress and Mental Workload EEG Dataset)
- **Reference:** Ghosh et al., 2022
- **Participants:** 40 healthy subjects (aged 18–30)
- **Acquisition Hardware:** Emotiv EPOC+ 14-channel wireless headset
- **Sampling Frequency:** 128 Hz
- **Electrode Placement (10–20 System):**
  - Frontal / Pre-frontal: `AF3`, `F7`, `F3`, `FC5`, `FC6`, `F4`, `F8`, `AF4`
  - Temporal: `T7`, `T8`
  - Parietal: `P7`, `P8`
  - Occipital: `O1`, `O2`

## Cognitive Task Paradigms
1. **Relaxation (Baseline):** 
   - 2-minute resting state with eyes open, gazing at fixation cross. Serves as intra-subject cognitive baseline.
2. **Mental Arithmetic:**
   - Serial subtraction under time pressure (e.g. subtracting 7 or 13 continuously), eliciting cognitive workload.
3. **Mirror-Image Tracing:**
   - Visual-motor coordination task tracing complex geometric patterns with reversed visual feedback.
4. **Stroop Color-Word Test:**
   - Incongruent stimuli requiring response inhibition under strict reaction deadlines.

## Evaluation Setup
- Binary comparisons:
  - **Relax vs. Arithmetic** (Cognitive Workload)
  - **Relax vs. Mirror-Image** (Psychomotor Stress)
  - **Relax vs. Stroop** (Inhibitory Control Stress)
